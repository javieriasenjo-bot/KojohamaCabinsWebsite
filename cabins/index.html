Which cabin should you choose?

Ocean Stay Sol

Choose Sol for a bright blue-and-white interior, with one double bed and three single beds.

Ocean Stay Zen

Choose Zen for tatami flooring, a kotatsu and an additional futon alongside the bedroom beds. Maximum occupancy is five guests.

Ocean Stay Rustic

Choose Rustic for wood textures and a cosy interior. Rustic has no tatami or kotatsu; those features belong to Zen.

Travelling with children or planning a BBQ?

Children and infants are welcome. BBQ is available by prior arrangement for ¥3,500 per stay, with our grill provided; bring two compatible gas cartridges, food, utensils and other gear.

Sea views, shore fishing and local outings

Check-in: Zen from 14:00; Sol and Rustic from 15:00. Checkout is by 10:00 for all three cabins. Self-checkout is possible earlier at any hour. Check the arrival window on your booking platform.

Ayoro Onsen is 290 m away, about a four-minute walk. Kojohama Cafe is around 100 m away, about one minute on foot. Other onsens, restaurants and local food shops are within walking distance. Onsen entry is paid separately, and business hours vary. Sanlaiva Ski Resort is approximately 25–30 minutes by car; allow extra time in snow or ice and check the resort’s seasonal opening, rentals and lessons.

Every cabin has a portable induction stove, pots and pans, toaster, rice cooker, microwave with oven function, kitchenware and children’s tableware. A washer, laundry detergent, clothes drying rack, towels and bath toiletries are provided. Zen alone has a tatami living room, a winter kotatsu and an optional living-room futon, within its five-guest cap.

Swimming is prohibited at the beach in front of the cabins. Never enter the sea deeper than your knees; there are no lifeguards. Avoid the water’s edge in dangerous weather, at night or after drinking; children must not go alone. Enjoy shore walks and rod-and-line fishing, including surf casting and lure fishing, only where local rules permit. Follow species, season and area restrictions, posted notices, weather and sea conditions. Do not collect shellfish, sea urchins, crabs or seaweed; fishing rights and local regulations apply. Fishing equipment is not available for rent. Surfing is only for permitted local spots after checking regulations, access rules and sea conditions; seeing other surfers does not establish that a spot is safe or permitted.

Extra cleaning and service charges

  • No smoking anywhere on the property, indoors or outdoors, including e-cigarettes and heated tobacco. Cleaning and smell-removal fee: ¥100,000 including tax.
  • Pets are not allowed. Bringing a pet incurs a ¥100,000 cleaning fee including tax, including disposal of bedding and towels for the next guest. This pet fee does not apply to guide, hearing or assistance dogs. Damage is charged at actual cost.
  • Bringing a large amount of beach sand indoors: fixed additional cleaning fee of ¥20,000 including tax.
  • Unregistered overnight guests: ¥5,000 per unregistered person per night including tax. All guests must be included in the booking; the maximum remains five guests per cabin. This is separate from the normal booked extra-guest fee.
  • Checkout is by 10:00. Leaving late without notifying us beforehand: ¥5,000 including tax per 30 minutes, plus any cost caused to the next booking.
  • Leaving large items such as suitcases or furniture: disposal fee of ¥3,000–¥5,000 including tax, depending on size.
  • Soiling, damaging or losing furniture, equipment, keys or other property: actual cost, including damage caused by pets.
  • Forgotten-item returns within Japan only: ¥3,000 including tax per shipment, plus packaging. The guest pays postage on delivery. This is a service fee.

Before you arrive

Final terms apply from 1 November 2026; Japanese is the original. Owner-confirmed check-in: Zen 14:00; Sol and Rustic 15:00. Checkout by 10:00. Your booking platform’s cancellation, payment and refund conditions take priority.

Accommodation terms and house rules
  • Maximum five guests per cabin; register everyone staying. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by an adult parent, guardian or supervising adult. No pets except assistance dogs.
  • Complete the online pre-registration form before check-in: each guest’s name, address and contact details. Only foreign nationals without an address in Japan must provide nationality, passport number and a passport image. Key information may be withheld until registration is complete.
  • Staff and chatbot respond 09:00–19:00 Japan time; overnight replies are mainly automated and staff may reply after 09:00. For a lockout, leak or power outage, call the emergency number in the cabin manual at any time.
  • BBQ only in the designated area, by prior arrangement, and finish by 21:00. Quiet after 22:00 indoors and outdoors; no parties or events. Personal photos/social posts are welcome; commercial filming and drones require prior written consent.
  • December–March: leave the air conditioner on heating at 16°C when going out or checking out to prevent freezing. Turn off other appliances and lights, close windows and lock the door.
  • One designated parking space per cabin; ask before bringing more cars. No parcel acceptance or storage of valuables. Forgotten items are kept seven days including the discovery day, then handed to police; unsuitable perishables may be disposed of.
  • No swimming at the beach in front of the cabins; never enter deeper than your knees. No lifeguards. Avoid the water’s edge in high waves, strong winds, bad weather, at night or after drinking. Children must not go alone. After an earthquake or tsunami warning/advisory, leave immediately and follow the manual’s evacuation instructions.
  • Pay accommodation, cleaning and taxes through the booking platform in advance; no payment at the property. No separate child rates. Accommodation tax is collected as a resort fee on the platform. Any additional claim follows the platform’s process.
  • For direct bookings only: cancellation 15 or more days before arrival 0%; 8–14 days 50%; 2–7 days 80%; one day before, same day or no-show 100%. A shortened stay is charged one night (the first night). Platform bookings follow the cancellation policy shown at booking instead. Japanese law applies; the Sapporo District Court is the first-instance court.
