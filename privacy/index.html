Privacy policy

Learn how Ocean Stay Kojohama uses website analytics, booking-link and enquiry information, including Metricool, and how to contact Kagami Planning.

Optional analytics and your choice

Google Analytics (installed through Google Tag Manager) and Metricool are optional. In every region they remain off until you choose Accept analytics. Declining does not prevent you from browsing the site, using the stay estimator or following booking and contact links. Advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalisation remain denied even when you accept analytics.

If you accept, these services may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device information, visited page URLs, referring pages and interaction events. Google Analytics may use cookies; Metricool states that its tracker does not use cookies. Metricool privacy policy.

Your analytics choice is stored in this browser’s local storage for six months. You can change it at any time using Analytics settings in the footer. Declining later stops further optional tracking and clears the analytics cookies this site can access; it does not undo data already sent. Cloudflare also provides the hosting infrastructure and may process connection information to deliver and protect the site.

How we use it

We use aggregated information to understand website performance, improve travel information and measure clicks to booking platforms, reviews, contact links and language choices. We do not sell personal information.

External services

Bookings and payments through the booking links take place on Airbnb and are governed by their own privacy policies. Embedded Google Maps, Instagram and other external links are governed by their respective providers.

Enquiries

If you write to us by email or LINE, we use your message and contact details only to reply and to arrange your stay. Messages sent to our LINE account are forwarded by our system to our email and LINE account so that a person can answer them; email delivery is handled by an email-service provider. Guest reviews shown on cabin pages are quoted from public Airbnb and Booking.com listings.
