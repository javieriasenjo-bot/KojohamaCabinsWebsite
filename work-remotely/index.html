For digital nomads and remote workers

A whole cabin, a quiet coast, and room to focus.

Ocean Stay Kojohama suits people who work from anywhere and want more than a hotel room: a private cabin with fast Wi-Fi (about 100 Mbps), a full kitchen and a washing machine, beside the Pacific and within easy reach of New Chitose Airport.

What makes a longer stay work

The whole cabin is yours

No shared rooms or lobbies — calls and deep work happen on your schedule.

Fast Wi-Fi — about 100 Mbps

Our own speed test measured about 100 Mbps download and upload: enough for video calls and large file transfers.

A full kitchen

Cook with local seafood and groceries — it matters on a stay of a week or more.

A washing machine

Pack light and wash as you go.

Comfortable in every season, including Hokkaido's winter.

Free dedicated parking

One space per cabin, right by the door.

A working week in Kojohama

Morning

Walk the shoreline before the first call. The coast is quiet, and the light changes every day.

Lunch

A tarako bowl at Tarakoya Kojohama or seafood at Tatchan Shokudo — both a short drive away.

After work

Soak at Ayoro Onsen, Kojohama Onsen Hotel or the 24-hour Hana no Yu, then gelato at Yu Gelato.

Weekend

Upopoy National Ainu Museum is about 25 minutes by car, and Noboribetsu Onsen is close by.

Supplies nearby

Seicomart near Kojohama Station covers everyday basics. For a full grocery shop, Co-op Sapporo Noboribetsu East is about 3.3 km from the station. Local tarako and fish shops sell seafood to cook at home. See all nearby essentials →

Getting here

About 55 minutes by car from New Chitose Airport. JR Kojohama Station is roughly a 13-minute walk from the cabins. A rental car is recommended for exploring, as local public transport is infrequent.
