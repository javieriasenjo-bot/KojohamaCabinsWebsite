Extra cleaning and service charges

1. No smoking anywhere on the property, indoors or outdoors, including e-cigarettes and heated tobacco. Cleaning and smell-removal fee: ¥100,000 excluding tax.
2. Pets are not allowed. Bringing a pet incurs a ¥100,000 cleaning fee excluding tax, including disposal of bedding and towels for the next guest. This pet fee does not apply to guide, hearing or assistance dogs. Damage is charged at actual cost.
3. Bringing a large amount of beach sand indoors: fixed additional cleaning fee of ¥20,000 excluding tax.
4. Unregistered overnight guests: ¥5,000 per unregistered person per night. All guests must be included in the booking; the maximum remains five guests per cabin. This is separate from the normal booked extra-guest fee.
5. Checkout is by 10:00. Leaving late without notifying us beforehand: ¥5,000 including tax per 30 minutes, plus any cost caused to the next booking.
6. Leaving large items such as suitcases or furniture: disposal fee of ¥3,000–¥5,000 depending on size.
7. Soiling, damaging or losing furniture, equipment, keys or other property: actual cost, including damage caused by pets.
8. Forgotten-item returns within Japan only: ¥3,000 including tax per shipment, plus packaging. The guest pays postage on delivery. This is a service fee.

For bookings through Airbnb or other booking platforms, claims are submitted through the platform’s own process and payment is subject to its rules and decision.
