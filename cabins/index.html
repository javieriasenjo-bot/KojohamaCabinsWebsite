Find the Kojohama cabin that fits your stay.

All three cabins offer privacy, ocean views, a kitchen, Wi-Fi, heating, air conditioning, a washer and free parking. Which cabin should you choose?

Ocean Stay Sol

Choose Sol for a bright blue-and-white interior, with one double bed and three single beds.

Ocean Stay Zen

Choose Zen for tatami flooring, a kotatsu and an additional futon alongside the bedroom beds. Maximum occupancy is five guests.

Ocean Stay Rustic

Choose Rustic for wood textures and a cosy interior. Rustic has no tatami or kotatsu; those features belong to Zen.

Travelling with children or planning a BBQ?

Children and infants are welcome. BBQ is available by prior arrangement for ¥3,500 per stay, with our grill provided; bring two compatible gas cartridges, food, utensils and other gear.

Sea views, shore fishing and local outings

Check-in: Zen from 14:00; Sol and Rustic from 15:00. Checkout is by 10:00 for all three cabins. Self-checkout is possible earlier at any hour. Check the arrival window on your booking platform.

Ayoro Onsen is 290 m away, about a four-minute walk. Kojohama Cafe is around 100 m away, about one minute on foot. Other onsens, restaurants and local food shops are within walking distance. Onsen entry is paid separately, and business hours vary. Sanlaiva Ski Resort is approximately 25–30 minutes by car; allow extra time in snow or ice and check the resort’s seasonal opening, rentals and lessons.

Every cabin has a portable induction stove, pots and pans, toaster, rice cooker, microwave with oven function, kitchenware and children’s tableware. A washer, laundry detergent, clothes drying rack, towels and bath toiletries are provided. Zen alone has a tatami living room, a winter kotatsu and an optional living-room futon, within its five-guest cap.

The sea directly outside the cabins is not for swimming or entering the water at any time of year. Enjoy shore walks and rod-and-line fishing, including surf casting and lure fishing, only where local rules permit. Follow species, season and area restrictions, posted notices, weather and sea conditions. Do not collect shellfish, sea urchins, crabs or seaweed; fishing rights and local regulations apply. Fishing equipment is not available for rent. Surfing is only for permitted local spots after checking regulations, access rules and sea conditions; seeing other surfers does not establish that a spot is safe or permitted.

Extra cleaning and service charges

  • No smoking anywhere on the property, indoors or outdoors, including e-cigarettes and heated tobacco. Cleaning and smell-removal fee: ¥100,000 excluding tax.
  • Pets are not allowed. Bringing a pet incurs a ¥100,000 cleaning fee excluding tax, including disposal of bedding and towels for the next guest. This pet fee does not apply to guide, hearing or assistance dogs. Damage is charged at actual cost.
  • Bringing a large amount of beach sand indoors: fixed additional cleaning fee of ¥20,000 excluding tax.
  • Unregistered overnight guests: ¥5,000 per unregistered person per night. All guests must be included in the booking; the maximum remains five guests per cabin. This is separate from the normal booked extra-guest fee.
  • Checkout is by 10:00. Leaving late without notifying us beforehand: ¥5,000 including tax per 30 minutes, plus any cost caused to the next booking.
  • Leaving large items such as suitcases or furniture: disposal fee of ¥3,000–¥5,000 depending on size.
  • Soiling, damaging or losing furniture, equipment, keys or other property: actual cost, including damage caused by pets.
  • Forgotten-item returns within Japan only: ¥3,000 including tax per shipment, plus packaging. The guest pays postage on delivery. This is a service fee.

For bookings through Airbnb or other booking platforms, claims are submitted through the platform’s own process and payment is subject to its rules and decision.
