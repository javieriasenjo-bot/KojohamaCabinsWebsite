Noboribetsu from Kojohama

Volcanic landscapes and onsen, with the coast as your base.

Ocean Stay Kojohama makes a convenient, quieter base for a day in Noboribetsu. Driving is strongly recommended because local public transport is infrequent. Allow roughly 20–30 minutes by car to the Noboribetsu Onsen area, depending on traffic, weather and your exact destination.

A simple half-day or full-day route

1. Jigokudani (Hell Valley)

Begin at the dramatic volcanic crater and observation area. The official tourism association lists admission as free and paid parking beside the site. Visit in daylight for the clearest views and check trail conditions before longer walks.

2. Onsen town

Walk through the compact hot-spring district for lunch, shops or a day-use bath. Bathing hours and admission rules differ by hotel, so confirm them directly before leaving Kojohama.

3. Forest walk or footbath

If conditions permit, continue toward Oyunuma and the natural river footbath. Trails can be wet, snowy or seasonally restricted; wear suitable shoes and follow posted closures.

4. Return to the coast

Come back to Kojohama for seafood, a local onsen or a quiet evening at your cabin. This avoids changing accommodation while combining two distinct Hokkaido landscapes.

Practical planning

By car

Use current navigation on the day and allow extra time in rain, snow or holiday traffic. Winter tyres are necessary in winter. Four-wheel drive is not normally required for the cabins.

Without a car

Train and bus combinations are possible, but services are limited. Check JR and local bus timetables for the exact date; do not assume frequent connections or a late return.

Parking

Jigokudani has paid parking close to the observation area. The official page currently notes a shared ticket arrangement with Oyunuma parking outside winter, but details can change.

Before departure

Check weather, trail notices, day-bath hours and the last transport connections. Keep a shorter plan available when winter conditions make forest paths unsuitable.

Confirm current information with the Noboribetsu International Tourism and Convention Association. Hokkaido’s official tourism site also publishes a Noboribetsu–Kojohama driving itinerary.
