Ocean Stay Zen

Ocean Stay Zen is a Japanese-style cabin on the Kojohama coast in Shiraoi, Hokkaido. Simple lines, deep-indigo details and an open lounge sit with the constant presence of the Pacific. A loft-style sleeping space and a displayed samurai armour set give this Hokkaido cabin its unmistakable zen identity.

Best for families or groups wanting the flexible loft-style sleeping layout and a calm Japanese-inspired interior.
