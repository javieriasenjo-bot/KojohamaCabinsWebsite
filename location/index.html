Location & access

Easy to reach. Quiet when you arrive.

Ocean Stay sits on the Kojohama coast between Shiraoi and Noboribetsu. A rental car is the easiest way to explore the area, although Kojohama Station is within walking distance.

By train and on foot

Kojohama Station is the nearest JR station, about 650–700 metres away and roughly a 13-minute walk. Local services are infrequent, so check the timetable carefully. For a flexible stay, we strongly recommend renting a car.

Taxi

A local taxi company serves Kojohama. Shiraoi Kotsu publishes dispatch service on 0144-82-2244. Availability and operating times can change, so reserve or reconfirm first.

Parking

Each of the three cabins has its own free parking space, right by the cabin. There are also two extra spaces beside the Rustic cabin, so if you are arriving with a second car, just let us know.

Winter arrival

Use winter tires from the start of the cold season. Four-wheel drive is not required. The main road is cleared frequently; cabin parking areas are cleared as needed. Allow more time after snowfall.

Check-in and luggage

Check-in is from 4 PM to 2 AM and checkout is before 10 AM. There is no dedicated luggage storage. Advance requests may sometimes be accommodated while cleaning is underway, but cannot be guaranteed.

Finding the entrance

Enter the full address into your navigation app and keep your arrival instructions available. If arriving after dark or in snow, approach slowly and look for the three cabins together beside the coast.

Tarakoya Kojohama

A tarako and seafood shop with a dining room at 185-7 Kojohama — take-home tarako, mentaiko and dried fish, plus tarako bowls. It has regular closing days, so check before visiting.
