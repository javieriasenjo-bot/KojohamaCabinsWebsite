Kojohama food guide

Taste Kojohama through tarako, seafood and spring-water trout.

Kojohama’s food story begins with the Pacific. Tarako—salted cod roe—is the best-known local specialty, alongside seasonal seafood and trout raised in the spring waters connected with Lake Kuttara.

Three tastes to look for

Kojohama tarako

Try it over rice, in Japanese-style pasta or as a take-home product. Tarakoya Kojohama is a long-established local stop with seafood sales and dining.

Seasonal seafood

Local restaurants and fish shops serve what is available from the coast. Tacchan Shokudo is known for seafood bowls; menus and availability change.

Local trout

Trout farms use clear local spring water and may cook your catch as sashimi, fried fish or salt-grilled fish.

Plan without disappointment

Many small businesses close on particular weekdays, finish lunch service early or sell out. Check opening hours on the day and keep an alternative nearby. Browse the official Kojohama dining directory →
