Shiraoi culture guide

Meet a living culture through Ainu voices and perspectives.

Shiraoi is an important place to learn about Ainu history, language and contemporary culture. The most meaningful approach is to listen to Ainu perspectives, follow local guidance and understand that this is a living culture—not only a story of the past.

Begin with the National Ainu Museum

The permanent exhibition presents language, history, worldview, everyday life, work and exchange from Ainu perspectives. Give the museum enough time before joining park programs.

Listen first

Use the museum’s own interpretation and Ainu voices as your primary context. Avoid treating sacred practices, designs or objects as decorative curiosities.

Culture continues today

Traditional knowledge is passed on while artists, speakers and communities also create contemporary expressions of Ainu identity and culture.

Follow photography rules

Photography and video restrictions apply in some museum and performance spaces. Check signs and staff guidance before recording.

Extend the learning beyond one visit

Attend cultural programs, explore the kotan and craft spaces, and support work presented through authorized museum and community channels. Check the official Upopoy program calendar →
