Two-day Shiraoi itinerary

Two unhurried days of culture, coast, seafood and onsen.

This itinerary keeps travel time sensible and leaves room to enjoy the cabins. A car is recommended because local public transport is infrequent, while JR remains possible with careful timetable planning.

Day one: Upopoy and Shiraoi

Morning

Drive about 25 minutes to Upopoy. Begin at the National Ainu Museum, then choose one or two park programs rather than rushing through everything.

Afternoon

Allow time around Lake Poroto or central Shiraoi. Confirm museum reservations, opening days and seasonal schedules before departure.

Evening

Return to Kojohama for a simple meal, a nearby onsen and a quiet evening at your cabin.

Day two: stay close to the sea

Coastal morning

Walk around Ayoro’s coast, shrine and lighthouse viewpoints. In winter, check wind and ice conditions.

Local lunch

Try seafood, Kojohama tarako or local trout. Opening days vary, so keep a second option in mind.

Flexible afternoon

Choose another onsen, Nachu no Mori, or simply slow down at the cabin. Keep the itinerary weather-friendly.

Use the official Shiraoi Tourism Association model course and each venue’s website to confirm current details.
