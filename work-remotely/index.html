For digital nomads and remote workers

A whole cabin, a quiet coast, and room to focus.

Ocean Stay Kojohama suits people who work from anywhere and want more than a hotel room: a private cabin with Wi-Fi (approximately 100 Mbps in a 2025 measurement; speeds vary), a full kitchen and a washing machine, beside the Pacific and within easy reach of New Chitose Airport.

A working week in Kojohama

Morning

Walk the shoreline before the first call. The coast is quiet, and the light changes every day.

Lunch

A tarako bowl at Tarakoya Kojohama or seafood at Tatchan Shokudo — both a short drive away.

After work

Soak at Ayoro Onsen, Kojohama Onsen Hotel or the 24-hour Hana no Yu, then gelato at Yu Gelato.

Weekend

Upopoy National Ainu Museum is about 25 minutes by car, and Noboribetsu Onsen is close by.

Supplies nearby

Seicomart near Kojohama Station covers everyday basics. For a full grocery shop, Co-op Sapporo Noboribetsu East is about 3.3 km from the station. Local tarako and fish shops sell seafood to cook at home. See all nearby essentials →

Getting here

About 60–80 minutes by car from New Chitose Airport. Allow extra time for winter weather. JR Kojohama Station is roughly a 13-minute walk from the cabins. A rental car is recommended for exploring, as local public transport is infrequent.
