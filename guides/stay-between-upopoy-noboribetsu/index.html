Travel guide

A quiet base between Upopoy and Noboribetsu.

Kojohama suits travellers who want cultural visits and volcanic landscapes during the day, then a private cabin and the sound of the Pacific in the evening.

What makes the location useful

Upopoy to the east

Allow roughly 25 minutes by car from Ocean Stay. Begin with the National Ainu Museum, then leave time for the park rather than treating it as a quick stop.

Noboribetsu to the west

Noboribetsu Station and Marine Park NIXE are nearby; the onsen district and Jigokudani sit farther inland. A car makes the combination straightforward.

Kojohama at the centre

The village offers coast, seafood and local onsen without the pace of a large resort area. It is a place to return to, not another attraction to rush through.

Who will enjoy this base

Couples and small groups

Three distinct cabins make it easier to choose a mood and layout rather than a standard hotel room.

Self-drive travellers

A car is the most flexible choice because local train services are infrequent. Free shared parking is available at the property.

Slow-season visitors

From October to March, a coastal base makes it easy to alternate outdoor plans with onsen, food and quiet time indoors.

Upopoy is about 500 metres from JR Shiraoi Station. Noboribetsu’s official guide notes that the onsen district is about 8 kilometres from JR Noboribetsu Station. Check current schedules and road conditions before travel. Check official Noboribetsu access information →
