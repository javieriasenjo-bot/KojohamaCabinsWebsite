Accommodation terms and house rules

Terms and conditions of accommodation for Ocean Stay Kojohama (Sol, Zen and Rustic), operated by Kagami Planning LLC. Effective 1 November 2026.

The Japanese text is the original (日本語); this English text is a translation. If you booked through Airbnb, Booking.com, Trip.com or another booking site, the cancellation policy, payment and refund conditions shown there take priority (Article 1, paragraph 3).

Effective: 1 November 2026

Article 1 (Scope of Application)

Accommodation contracts, and related contracts, concluded between guests and the accommodation facility “Ocean Stay Kojohama” operated by Kagami Planning LLC (the “Company”) (consisting of the Sol, Zen and Rustic houses; address: 595 Aza-Kojohama, Shiraoi-cho, Shiraoi-gun, Hokkaido; inn business licenses: Sol – Tanto-sei No. 39-5, Zen – Tanto-sei No. 39, Rustic – Tanto-sei No. 39-6; hereinafter the “Facility”) are governed by these Terms and Conditions. Matters not provided for in these Terms and Conditions are governed by laws and regulations or by generally established practice.

2 If the Facility agrees to a special arrangement that does not conflict with laws, regulations or established practice, that special arrangement takes precedence over the preceding paragraph.

3 For accommodation contracts booked through an online booking site (a “Booking Site”), the cancellation policy, payment method and other conditions shown by the Facility on the Booking Site apply with priority as a special arrangement under the preceding paragraph. This also applies where the handling of cancellations differs between these Terms and Conditions and the Booking Site depending on the stay period, the booking date, the rate plan or other factors; in that case the Booking Site’s terms prevail.

Article 2 (Application for an Accommodation Contract)

A person applying for an accommodation contract with the Facility is asked to provide the following:

  1. The names of the guests and the number of guests
  2. The dates of stay and the expected time of arrival
  3. The accommodation charges (as displayed on the Booking Site or elsewhere at the time of booking)
  4. Any other information the Facility considers necessary

2 If a guest asks, during the stay, to extend the stay beyond the dates in item 2 of the preceding paragraph, the Facility will treat that request as a new application for an accommodation contract made at the time of the request.

3 Each house accommodates a maximum of 5 guests. Persons other than the guests on the booking may not stay. Guests may not stay with pets (except service dogs for persons with disabilities, such as guide dogs, hearing dogs and assistance dogs).

4 Stays by minors only (persons under 18 years of age) are not accepted. Minors must be accompanied by an adult parent, guardian or supervising adult.

Article 3 (Conclusion of the Accommodation Contract)

An accommodation contract is concluded when the Facility accepts the application under the preceding article, unless it is proven that the Facility did not accept it.

2 Once the contract is concluded, the guest must pay the full accommodation charges in advance, by the method and the deadline specified by the Facility (“Advance Payment”). For bookings made through a Booking Site, payment on the Booking Site is the Advance Payment.

3 The Advance Payment is applied first to the accommodation charges finally payable by the guest. If Article 6 or Article 18 applies, it is applied next to the cancellation charge and then to compensation for damage, and any balance is refunded.

4 If the Advance Payment under paragraph 2 is not made by the deadline set by the Facility, the accommodation contract becomes void, provided that the Facility informed the guest of this when setting the deadline.

5 Advance Payment applies both to direct bookings with the Facility and to bookings made through a Booking Site.

Article 4 (Special Arrangement Waiving Advance Payment)

Notwithstanding paragraph 2 of the preceding article, the Facility may, in individual cases, agree to a special arrangement under which Advance Payment is not required.

2 If, when accepting an application, the Facility did not request Advance Payment or did not set a deadline for it, the Facility is treated as having agreed to the special arrangement in the preceding paragraph.

Article 4-2 (Request for Cooperation with Infection Prevention Measures)

The Facility may ask persons intending to stay to cooperate with infection prevention measures, in accordance with Article 4-2, paragraph 1 of the Inns and Hotels Act.

Article 5 (Refusal to Conclude an Accommodation Contract)

The Facility may decline to conclude an accommodation contract in the following cases. This does not mean that the Facility will refuse a contract in any case other than those provided for in Article 5 of the Inns and Hotels Act.

  1. The application is not made in accordance with these Terms and Conditions.
  2. No room is available because the Facility is fully booked.
  3. The person intending to stay is considered likely to act, in connection with the stay, in violation of laws or regulations, public order or good morals.
  4. The person intending to stay is considered to fall under any of the following:
    1. An organized crime group, a member, quasi-member or associate of such a group as defined in Article 2, items 2 and 6 of the Act on Prevention of Unjust Acts by Organized Crime Group Members, or any other antisocial force
    2. A corporation or other organization whose business activities are controlled by an organized crime group or its members
    3. A corporation with an officer who is a member of an organized crime group
  5. The person intending to stay has behaved in a way that causes serious nuisance to other guests.
  6. The person intending to stay is a patient with a specified infectious disease or a similar person.
  7. The person intending to stay engages in violent demands or demands a burden beyond a reasonable extent (including repeated specified demands as defined in the Inns and Hotels Act). Requests for the removal of social barriers under the Act for Eliminating Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities do not fall under this item.
  8. The Facility cannot provide accommodation because of a natural disaster, a breakdown of facilities or other unavoidable reasons.
  9. Under the Hokkaido Ordinance for Enforcement of the Inns and Hotels Act, the person intending to stay is considered to fall under either of the following:
    1. The person is heavily intoxicated or behaves in a markedly abnormal way and is likely to cause nuisance to other guests.
    2. The person’s clothing or belongings are markedly unclean and would interfere with other guests’ hygiene.

Article 5-2 (Explanation of a Refusal)

If the Facility declines to conclude an accommodation contract under the preceding article, the person who intended to stay may ask for an explanation of the reason.

Article 6 (Guest’s Right to Cancel)

Guests may cancel an accommodation contract by notifying the Facility.

2 If a guest cancels all or part of an accommodation contract for reasons attributable to the guest, the Facility will charge a cancellation fee as set out in Appendix 1 (except where the guest cancels before the Advance Payment deadline under Article 3, paragraph 2, and before payment has been made). However, for contracts booked through a Booking Site, the cancellation policy shown on the Booking Site at the time of booking applies with priority over Appendix 1, under Article 1, paragraph 3.

3 If a guest does not arrive by 12:00 midnight (0:00 the following day) on the day of the stay without contacting the Facility, the contract is deemed to have been cancelled by the guest. In this case, it is treated as a same-day cancellation and the same-day cancellation fee in Appendix 1 applies. For consecutive nights, the following nights are handled in accordance with Appendix 1 or the Booking Site’s cancellation policy.

Article 7 (Facility’s Right to Cancel)

The Facility may cancel an accommodation contract in the following cases. This does not mean that the Facility will refuse accommodation in any case other than those provided for in Article 5 of the Inns and Hotels Act.

  1. The guest is considered likely to act, or to have acted, in connection with the stay, in violation of laws or regulations, public order or good morals.
  2. The guest is considered to fall under Article 5, item 4.
  3. The guest has behaved in a way that causes serious nuisance to other guests.
  4. The guest is a patient with a specified infectious disease or a similar person.
  5. The guest has engaged in an act described in Article 5, item 7.
  6. The Facility cannot provide accommodation because of a natural disaster or other force majeure.
  7. The guest is considered to fall under a case described in Article 5, item 9.
  8. The guest does not comply with the prohibitions in Appendix 2, section 1 (those necessary for fire prevention), such as smoking in the house, tampering with fire-fighting equipment, or using fire outside designated areas.

2 If the Facility cancels the contract under the preceding paragraph, it will not charge for accommodation services that the guest has not yet received.

Article 7-2 (Explanation of a Cancellation)

If the Facility cancels an accommodation contract under the preceding article, the guest may ask for an explanation of the reason.

Article 8 (Guest Registration)

As the Facility has no staffed front desk, guests must register the following by check-in, by answering the online pre-registration form (questionnaire form) that the Facility sends after booking:

  1. The name, address and contact details of every guest
  2. For foreign nationals without an address in Japan, their nationality and passport number
  3. Any other information the Facility considers necessary

2 Foreign nationals without an address in Japan must attach an image of their passport to the pre-registration form in the preceding paragraph. The Facility keeps a copy.

3 The Facility may withhold the key information for the house until the registration in the preceding two paragraphs is complete.

Article 9 (Hours of Use)

Guests may use the house from 15:00 on the day of arrival until 10:00 the next morning. For consecutive nights, guests may use the house all day except on the days of arrival and departure.

2 Notwithstanding the preceding paragraph, the Facility may agree to use outside these hours. The charge in that case will be advised each time.

3 If a guest checks out after 10:00 without prior notice, a cleaning staff waiting fee of JPY 5,000 (tax included) per 30 minutes will be charged. If the delay prevents cleaning from being finished in time and causes costs relating to the next booking, those costs may also be charged.

Article 10 (Compliance with the House Rules)

Within the buildings and grounds of the Facility, guests must follow the “House Rules” in Appendix 2. The same applies to notices given in the House Manual, on the Booking Site or posted in the house.

Article 11 (Inquiry Hours and Hours of Use of Facilities)

Inquiries to the Facility (through Booking Site messages or otherwise) are handled as follows. The hours of use of outdoor facilities (barbecue equipment, sauna, etc.) are set out in Appendix 2.

  1. 9:00–19:00 (Japan time): our chatbot and staff respond.
  2. 19:00–9:00 the next morning (Japan time): mainly our chatbot responds. Except in emergencies, staff replies may come after 9:00 the next morning.

2 In an emergency, such as a lockout, a water leak or a power outage, please call the emergency number in the House Manual at any time.

3 The hours in the preceding paragraph may be changed temporarily when unavoidable. In that case, guests will be notified by appropriate means.

Article 12 (Payment of Charges)

The charges payable by the guest are the accommodation charges, cleaning fee and taxes displayed on the Booking Site or elsewhere at the time of booking. Accommodation tax is collected on the Booking Site as a “resort fee”. There are no separate rates by age (no child rates).

2 The charges in the preceding paragraph are paid at the time of booking, by the payment method of the Booking Site (or, for direct bookings with the Facility, by the payment method the Facility specifies). Payment at the Facility is not accepted.

3 Once the Facility has provided the house and it is available for use, the accommodation charges are payable even if the guest chooses not to stay.

4 Additional charges, cancellation fees and compensation for damage arising during or after the stay are payable through the Booking Site’s procedures for bookings made through a Booking Site, and by the method and deadline the Facility specifies for direct bookings.

Article 13 (Liability of the Facility)

If the Facility causes damage to a guest in performing, or by failing to perform, the accommodation contract or a related contract, the Facility will compensate for that damage, unless the damage is not attributable to the Facility.

2 The beach and sea in front of the Facility are outside its grounds and are not managed by the Facility. The Facility is not liable for accidents on the beach or in the sea (accidents while in the water, accidents caused by high waves, falls, etc.), except where caused by the Facility’s intent or negligence.

3 If part of the equipment is temporarily unavailable because of a natural disaster or other disaster, a power outage, water outage, communication failure (including Wi-Fi), equipment breakdown or other circumstances not attributable to the Facility, the Facility will not compensate for the resulting damage and will not refund accommodation charges. If these circumstances make it impossible to continue the stay, Articles 7 and 14 apply. For contracts booked through a Booking Site, notwithstanding this paragraph, the Booking Site’s rules on cancellations and refunds (including rules on disasters) apply with priority (Article 1, paragraph 3).

Article 14 (When the Contracted House Cannot Be Provided)

If the Facility cannot provide the contracted house, it will, with the guest’s consent, arrange other accommodation on the same terms as far as possible.

2 If, notwithstanding the preceding paragraph, the Facility cannot arrange other accommodation, it will pay the guest compensation equal to the cancellation fee, and this compensation will be applied to any damages. However, no compensation is paid if the Facility is not responsible for being unable to provide the house.

Article 15 (Handling of Guests’ Belongings)

As the Facility has no staffed front desk, it does not take custody of belongings, cash or valuables. Guests are responsible for managing their own belongings.

2 If belongings brought into the Facility by a guest (excluding the valuables in the next paragraph) are lost or damaged through the Facility’s intent or negligence, the Facility will compensate for the damage, up to a maximum of JPY 30,000, except in cases of intent or gross negligence by the Facility.

3 For cash, securities, precious metals and other valuables, if the guest did not declare their type and value to the Facility in advance, the Facility is not liable for their loss or damage, in accordance with Article 597 of the Commercial Code. This does not apply where the loss or damage is caused by the Facility’s intent or gross negligence, or in other cases provided for in that article.

Article 16 (Luggage and Belongings)

The Facility does not accept luggage or parcels sent to the Facility before the guest’s arrival.

2 If luggage or belongings are left at the Facility after check-out and the owner is identified, the Facility will contact the owner and ask for instructions. If the owner wishes the items to be sent, they will be sent cash on delivery to an address in Japan only, and a handling fee of JPY 3,000 (tax included) per shipment will be charged in addition to the postage (packaging materials are charged separately).

3 If there are no instructions from the owner, or the owner cannot be identified, the items will be kept for 7 days including the day they were found, and then handed to the nearest police station. Food, drinks and other items unsuitable for storage may be disposed of at the Facility’s discretion.

Article 17 (Parking)

Parking is limited to one car per house. Please contact us in advance if you need more spaces.

2 When a guest uses the Facility’s parking, the Facility only provides the space and does not take responsibility for managing the vehicle, whether or not the vehicle key has been deposited. However, the Facility will compensate for damage caused by its intent or negligence in managing the parking area.

Article 18 (Liability of Guests)

If the Facility suffers damage through the intent or negligence of a guest, the guest must compensate the Facility for that damage.

2 The charges set out in Appendix 2, section 8 are charged as the minimum amount of the damage in the preceding paragraph.

Article 19 (Language, Governing Law and Jurisdiction)

The Japanese text of these Terms and Conditions is the original. If there is any difference between the Japanese text and a translation, the Japanese text prevails.

2 These Terms and Conditions and the accommodation contract are governed by Japanese law. The Sapporo District Court has exclusive jurisdiction as the court of first instance for any dispute relating to them.

Appendix 2 – House Rules (Articles 7, 10, 11, 13 and 18)

Within the buildings and grounds of the Facility, guests must follow the rules below.

1. Prohibitions (necessary for fire prevention; Article 7, paragraph 1, item 8)

  1. Smoking anywhere on the property (indoors and outdoors), including cigarettes, heated tobacco, e-cigarettes and similar products
  2. Using candles, fireworks or any other open flame indoors (cooking only with the cooking equipment provided)
  3. Barbecuing outside the designated area, and using the barbecue equipment after 21:00
  4. Covering, moving, removing or disabling fire alarms, fire extinguishers or any other fire-fighting or safety equipment

2. Consideration for Neighbors

  1. Please keep quiet, both indoors and outdoors, after 22:00.
  2. Parties and events are not permitted.

3. Inside the House

  1. Shoes are not allowed inside. Please take them off at the entrance.
  2. Please do not bring sand inside. When you come back from the beach, brush the sand off your shoes, feet and belongings before entering.
  3. Please do not drag suitcases inside.
  4. Please do not take any items from the house.
  5. Please do not send parcels to the Facility (Article 16, paragraph 1).
  6. Pets are not permitted (except service dogs for persons with disabilities; Article 2, paragraph 3).

4. Garbage

  1. Please sort garbage into 3 types: burnable, cans and glass bottles, and PET bottles.
  2. Please do not leave garbage outside (crows will tear the bags).
  3. If a lot of garbage builds up, please contact us via chat in advance.
  4. Please do not leave suitcases, furniture or other large items behind.

5. Going Out and Check-out

  1. Turn off the lights and other appliances, close the windows and lock the front door.
  2. In winter (December to March), to prevent freezing, do not turn off the air conditioner when going out or leaving; keep it on heating at 16°C. Please also follow any other equipment instructions from the Facility.
  3. Check-out is by 10:00 (Article 9).

6. Parking

  1. Parking is limited to one car per house in the designated space. Please do not park on neighboring land or on the road (Article 17).

7. Outdoor Facilities

  1. The barbecue equipment may be used until 21:00.
  2. The sauna (once installed) may be used from 7:00 to 21:00. The sauna is shared by the three houses. Please follow the separate “Sauna Rules” set by the Facility for how to use and book it and for safety precautions.

9. Using the Beach (Article 13, paragraph 2)

  1. Swimming is prohibited at the beach in front of the Facility. Do not go into the sea deeper than your knees.
  2. There are no lifeguards. When going near the water’s edge, check for your own safety.
  3. Do not go near the water’s edge in high waves, strong wind or bad weather, at night, or after drinking alcohol.
  4. Do not let children go to the beach alone.
  5. If you feel an earthquake, or if a tsunami warning or advisory is issued, leave the beach immediately and evacuate to the evacuation site shown in the House Manual.

10. Photography and Commercial Use

  1. Personal photos and posting on social media are welcome.
  2. The following require the Facility’s prior written consent (including by email). If consent is given, an additional charge may apply.
    1. Commercial photography or filming for advertising, TV programs, films, etc., and media coverage
    2. Photo shoots, parties, events, sales of goods or any other use other than accommodation
    3. Use of drones (the Civil Aeronautics Act and other laws must be observed)
  3. Please refrain from long live streams or anything else that may disturb the neighbors.
