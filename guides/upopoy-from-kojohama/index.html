Upopoy day-trip guide

A meaningful day with Ainu culture, from a quiet coastal base.

Upopoy National Ainu Museum and Park is one of Shiraoi’s essential visits. From Ocean Stay Kojohama it is approximately 25 minutes by car, allowing you to combine the museum and park with a peaceful evening beside the sea.

Getting there

By car

Driving is the most flexible option from the cabins and is recommended because local public transport is infrequent. Allow about 25 minutes, plus time for weather and traffic.

By train

Walk from the cabins to JR Kojohama Station, then travel to JR Shiraoi Station. Upopoy is approximately 500 metres—or about 10 minutes on foot—from Shiraoi Station. Check the JR timetable carefully.

How to shape the visit

Start with the museum

The National Ainu Museum provides context on Ainu history, language and culture. Some tickets, programs or workshops may require advance or same-day reservations.

Leave time for the park

Explore the traditional kotan, cultural performances and craft programs. Schedules, capacity and seasonal availability vary.

Return without rushing

Choose one or two priority programs, then allow time for the shop, café and walk back. In winter, finish before roads and paths become darker or icier.

Check the official Upopoy website for the current calendar, opening hours, admission and reservations. Photo courtesy of The Foundation for Ainu Culture.
