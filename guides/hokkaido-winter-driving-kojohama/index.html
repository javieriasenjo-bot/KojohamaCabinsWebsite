Travel guide

Driving to Kojohama in winter: a calm, practical plan.

A winter car gives you the freedom to connect Kojohama, Upopoy and Noboribetsu—but the day works best when you allow time for weather and shorter daylight.

Before leaving New Chitose Airport

Choose the right car

Book a vehicle fitted with winter tires. Four-wheel drive can add confidence, but winter tires and careful decisions matter more than the badge on the car.

Check the day, not only the forecast

Look at road conditions before departure and again after long stops. Snow, wind and temperature can change quickly along the coast.

Keep the first day simple

After a flight, drive directly to Kojohama rather than building a full sightseeing day around your arrival.

On the road and after arrival

Leave room for daylight

Treat driving times as planning ranges. Slow down, increase following distance and avoid a schedule that depends on arriving at the exact minute.

Parking at Ocean Stay

Each cabin has its own free parking space right at the door. The main road is cleared frequently; cabin parking is cleared as needed.

Build a weather-proof stay

Use clear days for Upopoy, Noboribetsu or the coast. Keep an onsen, a long cabin breakfast or a nearby meal as an easy alternative when conditions turn.

Upopoy publishes an airport-to-Shiraoi driving estimate of about 40 minutes to Shiraoi IC, followed by about 10 minutes to the park. Kojohama is farther west; always follow your live route and current road guidance. Check official Upopoy access information →
