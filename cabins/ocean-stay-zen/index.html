Children, infants and BBQ

Are children and infants welcome?

Yes. Children and infants are welcome in all three cabins, with a maximum of five guests per cabin. Ask us about any baby equipment you need before booking.

Can we use the BBQ grill?

Yes, by prior arrangement. Use of our Coleman RoadTrip Grill LXE-J II costs ¥3,500 per stay. Guests bring two compatible gas cartridges, food, utensils and other BBQ gear.

Sea views and local outings

Check-in: from 15:00 for all cabins. Checkout is by 10:00 for all three cabins. Self-checkout is possible earlier at any hour. Check the arrival window on your booking platform.

The beach is a 30-second walk from every cabin. Ayoro Onsen is 290 m away, about a four-minute walk. Kojohama Cafe is around 100 m away, about one minute on foot. Other onsens, restaurants and local food shops are within walking distance. Onsen entry is paid separately, and business hours vary. Sanlaiva Ski Resort is approximately 25–30 minutes by car; allow extra time in snow or ice and check the resort’s seasonal opening, rentals and lessons.

Every cabin has a portable induction stove, pots and pans, toaster, rice cooker, microwave with oven function, kitchenware and children’s tableware. A washer, laundry detergent, clothes drying rack, towels and bath toiletries are provided. Zen alone has a tatami living room and a winter kotatsu. Every cabin has the same beds: one double bed and three single beds in a bunk layout, for up to five guests.

The sea water in Kojohama is very cold throughout the year and it is not advisable to swim or surf in it. Please, follow the local regulations carefully. We strongly recommend you enjoy the nearby Onsens if you feel like going for a bath, instead of entering the sea.

Extra cleaning and service charges

  • No smoking anywhere on the property, indoors or outdoors, including e-cigarettes and heated tobacco. Cleaning and smell-removal fee: ¥100,000 including tax.
  • Pets are not allowed. Bringing a pet incurs a ¥100,000 cleaning fee including tax, including disposal of bedding and towels for the next guest. This pet fee does not apply to guide, hearing or assistance dogs. Damage is charged at actual cost.
  • Bringing a large amount of beach sand indoors: fixed additional cleaning fee of ¥20,000 including tax.
  • Unregistered overnight guests: ¥5,000 per unregistered person per night including tax. All guests must be included in the booking; the maximum remains five guests per cabin. This is separate from the normal booked extra-guest fee.
  • Checkout is by 10:00. Leaving late without notifying us beforehand: ¥5,000 including tax per 30 minutes, plus any cost caused to the next booking.
  • Leaving large items such as suitcases or furniture: disposal fee of ¥3,000–¥5,000 including tax, depending on size.
  • Soiling, damaging or losing furniture, equipment, keys or other property: actual cost, including damage caused by pets.
  • Forgotten-item returns within Japan only: ¥3,000 including tax per shipment, plus packaging. The guest pays postage on delivery. This is a service fee.

Effective 1 November 2026. For bookings through Airbnb or other booking platforms, claims are submitted through the platform’s own process and payment is subject to its rules and decision.
