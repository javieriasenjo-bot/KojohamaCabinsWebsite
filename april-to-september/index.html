April–September

Sea air, long evenings and green hills: Hokkaido from spring to early autumn.

April through September brings milder days, green hills and long evenings beside the Pacific. Choose a cabin with the atmosphere and sleeping layout that suits your trip, then check live availability on the cabin’s booking page.

April

Spring arrives later on Hokkaido’s coast than in much of Japan, and snow or ice can still appear early in the month. Check forecasts and road conditions before you travel.

May–June

Fresh greens return, and cherry blossoms reach Hokkaido later than most of Japan. Comfortable days for coastal walks and onsen visits.

July–August

Warm days by the sea. Summer is a busy travel season across Hokkaido, so book early. All three cabins have air-conditioning.

September

Cooler evenings return and autumn travel begins. Pack layers.
