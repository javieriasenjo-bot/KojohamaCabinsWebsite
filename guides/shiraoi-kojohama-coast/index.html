Shiraoi coast guide

Walk the Shiraoi coast, right from the cabin door.

Kojohama, in Shiraoi, Hokkaido, is a small village facing the open Pacific: black-sand beaches lined with tetrapods, a walk out towards the lighthouse, and a hilltop shrine that catches the sunrise. This page covers how to spend time on the Shiraoi coast and what to know about swimming, from the point of view of a private cabin that sits right on the shore.

Walking the Kojohama and Shiraoi coast

Ayoro beach and lighthouse

A black-sand shore with tetrapods and an easy walk towards the lighthouse (the lighthouse itself is closed to visitors). About 5 minutes by car from the cabins, or on foot or by bike. Go in daylight and follow posted access restrictions.

Sunrise from Kojohama Shrine

A hilltop shrine overlooking Kojohama and the Pacific, on the official local sightseeing route. About 3 minutes by car. On clear mornings the sun rises out of the sea.

The sea outside the cabin

All three Ocean Stay cabins face the sea, with the Pacific visible from the windows and terrace. A wet terrace mirroring the sky after rain, big waves on a sunny windy day, the first snow on the shore in winter: the sea changes every day even if you never leave the cabin.

Swimming: the Shiraoi coast is for watching and walking

The Shiraoi coast, including Kojohama, faces the open Pacific, and waves can be strong even on calm-looking days. As far as we know, Shiraoi has no lifeguarded swimming beach. Enjoy the coast from the shore, and if you want to swim, choose a beach with lifeguards.

SHIRAOI Beach summer event at Shiraoi port

A summer event run by SHIRAOI PROJECTS at Shiraoi port (third commercial port area). Swimming is not offered, but there is supervised rock-pool play, SUP sessions and a sea-creature pool made with local fishers. The 2025 edition ran from 21 July to 10 August; 2026 is planned for late July to early August. Check the organiser's announcements for dates.

Where to swim nearby: Usu beach (Date City)

A shallow, calm beach on Funka Bay. The 2026 swimming season runs from 18 July to 30 August, with the lifeguard station staffed 9:00–17:00. About 200 parking spaces (¥500 per car). About 20 minutes from Date IC on the Do-o Expressway; allow roughly an hour by car from Kojohama.

Staying safe by the sea

Keep off tetrapods, rocks and breakwaters. On rough or windy days, watch the waves from a distance. If you feel a strong earthquake or a tsunami warning is issued, leave the shore at once and move to high ground.

Frequently asked questions

Can you swim at Kojohama or elsewhere in Shiraoi?

The Shiraoi coast, including Kojohama, faces the open Pacific and waves can build quickly. As far as we know there is no lifeguarded swimming beach in Shiraoi. To swim, use a nearby beach with lifeguards, such as Usu beach in Date City.

Is there somewhere for children to enjoy the sea?

In summer, the SHIRAOI Beach event at Shiraoi port offers supervised rock-pool play and SUP sessions; swimming is not offered (2026 is planned for late July to early August). Check the organiser's announcements for dates.

How close are the cabins to the sea?

All three Ocean Stay cabins (Sol, Zen and Rustic) face the Pacific, with sea views from the windows and terrace. The Ayoro coast and lighthouse are about 5 minutes by car, and you can also walk there.
