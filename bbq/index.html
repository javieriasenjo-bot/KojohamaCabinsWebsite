Grill and supplies

Our grill
Use of our Coleman RoadTrip Grill LXE-J II is included in the fee.
Bring your own supplies
Bring both compatible gas cartridges, food, utensils and any other BBQ gear you need. Tongs, foil, seasonings and cooking oil are not promised as supplied equipment.

A few requests

No deep cleaning needed

Our cleaning staff handle the tedious grill disassembly and grease cleaning after checkout.

Wildlife precautions

To keep local wildlife away, please dispose of large leftover food scraps and close the lid after use.

Bring your favourite flavours

Please bring your own BBQ sauces, marinades and specialty spices.

Gas canisters: please bring your own

For hygiene and safety reasons, guests are required to bring their own gas canisters.

Required type

Coleman genuine OD canister (T-type, 470 g). Slim butane canisters (CB type) used for portable tabletop stoves are incompatible and cannot be used.

Required quantity: 2

Fit two compatible cartridges before use. The manufacturer requires both cartridge positions to be connected; do not operate the grill with only one cartridge.

Safety and use guide

Read this before you light the grill. If you are unsure how to use the grill, do not use it, and contact us through the booking platform you used to reserve Ocean Stay.

1. Before you start

Use the grill outdoors only, on a stable, level surface, away from flames and flammable materials. Keep children away. The grill and nearby parts get very hot. Make sure the grease tray is installed and filled with water up to the MAX line.

2. Already assembled: unfold only

No assembly is needed. Do not unpack, assemble wheels or install the transport handle. Lift the body fully using the leg and side handles, pull it toward you until the legs are fully open and lock the leg hook. Open both side tables, install the 9 V battery and add water to the grease tray up to MAX.

3. Connect the gas outdoors

Away from all flame sources, check the O-rings are undamaged, then screw each cartridge in horizontally, counter-clockwise, until it stops. Never force a cartridge or install it at an angle. Confirm both knobs are at OFF, hang the Twin Pack on the hook at the rear left, and connect the hose adapter to the grill’s rear adapter, turning the cover clockwise until it stops. Check for gas leaks.

4. Light and cook

Push a control knob in and turn it left toward HIGH, then press the ignition button and confirm the burner is lit. If it does not light, turn the knob fully to OFF, wait briefly and try again. Turn from HIGH toward OFF to lower the flame. Keep your face, hands and clothing away from the burners, and never move the grill while it is lit.

5. If something is wrong

Gas smell or suspected leak: turn both knobs fully to OFF, do not light the grill or operate electrical switches, and move away. Do not use damaged, leaking or improperly connected parts.

6. Turn off and cool down

Turn both knobs fully to OFF and confirm all flames are out. Let the grill cool completely before touching, cleaning, folding or storing it. Never remove a cartridge or store the grill near a flame, sparks or another heat source.

7. Cleanup

Once the grill is completely cool, disconnect the adapter, remove the cartridges and refit their caps. You do not need to clean the grill: our team handles the degreasing and cleaning after checkout.

This guide is a summary for guests and does not replace the manufacturer’s manual.
