Privacy policy

How Ocean Stay Kojohama handles website information.

Information we collect

This website may collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, visited pages and interaction events through Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics, installed through Google Tag Manager.

How we use it

We use aggregated information to understand website performance, improve travel information and measure clicks to booking platforms, reviews, contact links and language choices. We do not sell personal information.

External services

Bookings and payments take place on Airbnb or Ctrip (Trip.com Group) and are governed by their own privacy policies. Embedded Google Maps, Instagram and other external links are governed by their respective providers.

Enquiries

If you write to us by email or LINE, we use your message and contact details only to reply and to arrange your stay. Messages sent to our LINE account are forwarded by our system to our email and LINE account so that a person can answer them; email delivery is handled by an email-service provider. Guest reviews shown on cabin pages are quoted from public Airbnb and Booking.com listings.
